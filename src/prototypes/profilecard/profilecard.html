HUMAN UNDERNEATH

Resident Zero#0010 ✓

NomadNon-Fiction

Without a single photon of light to guide my eyes, my mind begins to fracture and play tricks. The sheer density of the blackness creates a terrifying form of sensory deprivation.

Motion stirs only the fabric draped around my absence. I feel the weight of my cloak, but not the place where it rests. My shadow casts a body I no longer possess.

My surroundings feel hostile. This place seems abandoned, yet dread creeps upon me with a silence that strangles.
